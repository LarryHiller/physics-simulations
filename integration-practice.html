Lab notebook — discussion questions
  1. What is the difference between an indefinite integral and a definite integral?
  2. What is the "+C" in an indefinite integral for? Why do two functions that only differ by a constant have the same derivative?
  3. What is the difference between geometric area and net-signed area under a function? When do they come out equal?
  4. By default, does evaluating a definite integral give you geometric area or net-signed area? How do you know?
  5. Why does integrating sin(kx) produce a negative cosine, but integrating cos(kx) produces a positive sine? Why does the sign only show up on one of the two?
  6. When you integrate the same function twice, why does the first constant of integration end up attached to an x, instead of just staying a lone number?

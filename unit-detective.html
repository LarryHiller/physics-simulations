Why units, not numbers

  1. Two terms are added together in your model. What does that force to be true about their units? Which term did you use as your anchor?
  2. The exponent of an exponential and the argument of a sine must be dimensionless. Explain to a classmate why, without using the word "rule."
  3. A coefficient came out as [y]/[x]³. Where does the 3 come from — the graph, or the algebra?

Reading the fit

  1. Your trial curve looked perfect but the residual showed a smooth wave. What does a patterned residual tell you that the overlay did not?
  2. You fit a higher-order polynomial and the leading coefficient came out near zero. What was the data telling you?
  3. In nonsense mode you could not recognise the units. What did you have to reason from instead?

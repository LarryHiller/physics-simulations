AP Physics · Unit 1 · Kinematics

Analytical Motion Graphs

One graph is given. Build the other two with middle school calculus: slopes to go down the stack, areas to go up.

Lab notebook — discussion questions

1. Why does going down the stack use slopes, and going up use areas? What would the units of a slope or an area tell you, even if you forgot the rule?

2. An area under the velocity graph is a change in position, ∆s. Why do you need the starting position to draw the position graph, but not to find ∆s?

3. When the velocity graph crosses the time axis, why do you split the area there? What happens to the position graph at that moment?

4. A straight, slanted velocity segment gives a curved position segment. How can you tell from the velocity graph whether the position curve bends up or down?

5. On a curved position segment, you measured two slopes. Why is one slope not enough to describe that segment?

6. Where on a position graph is the velocity zero? Where on a velocity graph is the acceleration zero?

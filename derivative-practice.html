Lab notebook — discussion questions
  1. Why does a secant line between two points on a position-time graph give an average velocity, while a tangent line gives an instantaneous velocity?
  2. If you pick your two secant points closer and closer together, what does the secant line start to look like? What does that suggest about how a derivative is defined?
  3. For a straight-line (linear) position-time graph, why is the secant slope the same no matter which two points you choose?
  4. When you differentiate a constant term, the term disappears rather than becoming zero-times-something. Why does that make sense graphically?
  5. Why does differentiating sin(kx) produce cos(kx), but differentiating cos(kx) produces negative sin(kx)? Why does the sign only show up on one of the two?

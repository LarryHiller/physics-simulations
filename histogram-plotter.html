Histogram Plotter

Turn your data into a histogram, superimpose a normal curve, and shade the 1σ–5σ ranges to see how closely your data follows it.

1 · Grid & scale

Leave any box blank and it is set automatically from your data. The bin width, left edge, and right edge are shared by every data set so their bars line up.

2 · Axis label

Left blank, the axis takes its label from the first data set you show. The count axis is always labeled "Frequency."

5 · Highlight ranges (σ)

Shades the range within k standard deviations of each data set's own mean, and compares it to the 68–95–99.7 rule.

Add a data table with at least 2 points to highlight ranges.
